ABOUT SHALINI RATHOD / CONFIDENCE, STYLING & PRESENCE

TED Speaker, Celebrity Stylist & Confidence Coach

Shalini Rathod is a TED speaker, confidence and communication coach, image consultant and celebrity stylist who helps people transform the way they present themselves, express their ideas and connect with others.

Through her work in personal styling, body language and communication, she has helped more than 9,000 people build greater confidence, clarity and presence, while growing an online community of over 600,000 people.

“Known for making confidence practical and learnable, Shalini believes that powerful presence is not about becoming someone else—it is about expressing who you are with such authenticity and conviction that attention is not demanded, but naturally earned.”
